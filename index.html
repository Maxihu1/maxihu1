🔥 Maxihu1

Maxihu1 is an immortal soul with controllable blue fire coming from his eyes.

He lost his human body long ago, but kept going and slowly found his humanity again by helping people.

Maxihu1 Backstory

Jhon was born as a normal human.

There was nothing unusual about him when he was born. He was just a baby, born into a family that, at first, seemed like any other. But his life became difficult very early.

His mother and father died when he was still very young. With nobody left to take care of him, Jhon became homeless. He had no proper home, no family to return to, and no one waiting for him at the end of the day.

He learned to survive however he could. Sometimes that meant doing illegal things. He stole when he needed food or money, and he learned quickly that the world was not always kind to people who had nothing.

For years, his life stayed that way. There were no huge adventures and no great destiny that he knew about. He was simply trying to make it through one day at a time.

Then, when Jhon was seventeen, everything changed.

One day, Jhon robbed a store. He ran away with whatever he had managed to take and started crossing the street. Then he heard the truck. There was no time to move. The truck hit him.

Jhon did not survive.

When he opened his eyes again, he was no longer in the overworld. He was in Hell. He immediately realized what had happened and panicked.

“No, please! I don't wanna die!” his voice shook as he begged.

The Devil watched him for a moment.

“Hmm... okay.” Jhon looked up, confused.

“But only if you do this little job for me.”

The Devil explained that some humans had escaped from Hell. They had managed to get back into the overworld, and the Devil wanted them brought back. He wanted their souls.

“You find them, take their souls, and bring them back to me.”

Jhon did not care about anything except staying alive.

“Okay... I'll do anything, please!” The Devil agreed.

Jhon was sent back to the overworld in his normal human body. For the moment, everything looked normal again. But Jhon had a job to do.

When Jhon returned, the Devil gave him a list. Jhon looked at the paper and smiled.

“Okay. This will be easy.”

He started rolling the paper out.

And kept rolling.

And kept rolling.

The list was about a kilometer long. Jhon stared at it.

“Fuck.”

It was gonna take a while.

And it did.

Jhon spent twenty-five years hunting down the people on the list.

Twenty-five years of searching.

Twenty-five years of finding people.

Twenty-five years of taking their souls.

But something strange happened during those years. Jhon never aged.

His body stayed exactly the same as it had been when he died at seventeen. His face didn't change. His voice didn't get older. Even after twenty-five years, he still looked and sounded like the same seventeen-year-old boy who had been hit by that truck.

He also discovered something else about his new existence.

He could be hurt.

If someone shot him, for example, it would still hurt. The pain was real. But the next day, the damage would be gone. It was almost like waking up from a terrible dream. He could remember the pain, but there would be no permanent injury left behind. Jhon slowly realized that death was no longer something he needed to worry about.

Eventually, after twenty-five years, he finished the list.

He returned every soul to the Devil.

The Devil looked at him.

“You did your job. You're free.”

Jhon had expected something else.

Maybe another task.

Maybe another condition.

But the Devil actually kept his word.

Jhon was free.

He was no longer the Devil's servant. However, the abilities he had gained remained with him.

Five years passed.

Jhon continued living in the overworld.

One day, he saw a house burning. The neighborhood itself was completely ordinary. It was just a normal place with normal houses and normal people. Jhon had seen fires before. He had seen terrible things before. To someone who could not die, watching a house burn was almost becoming a normal thing.

But this time, he noticed someone screaming.

A mother was standing outside. Her baby was still inside the house.

Jhon looked at the burning building.

For a moment, he thought about walking away.

“Well, I don't really care.”

He turned as if he was going to leave.

Then something inside him said no.

He stopped.

He didn't understand it.

Why this house?

Why this baby?

He had walked away from terrible things before. He had watched fires burn before. He had spent twenty-five years killing people for the Devil. So why couldn't he leave this time?

Jhon turned around.

Then he ran.

He rushed into the burning house.

The heat hit him immediately.

It hurt.

It hurt badly.

The flames burned him as he moved through the building, but he kept going.

He ran through the rooms, looking left and right. Nothing.

Then another room. Nothing. He kept searching until he finally found it.

The room with the baby. Jhon ran inside and grabbed the baby.

He didn't stop to think.

He just ran.

He carried the baby toward a window and jumped out.

The baby was safe.

Jhon wasn't.

His body was still burning. The pain was overwhelming. Then, after a while, he stopped feeling it. The mother rushed toward her baby and held them tightly.

She thanked Jhon again and again.

“Thank you! Thank you so much!”

Jhon didn't really know what to say.

“Yeah... okay.”

Then he went home.

He was still burning.

The next morning, Jhon expected his body to heal.

It always had before. He waited. Nothing happened.

He looked at himself.

His skin wasn't growing back.

At first, he thought maybe he simply needed more time.

But the hours passed.

Nothing.

Then he realized the truth.

There was no normal body left.

His skin was gone.

His face was gone.

His body was gone.

The only thing remaining was his soul.

It was black, like a dark silhouette that had somehow remained after everything else had been destroyed.

Jhon stared at himself.

“Why am I not dead?”

Then he remembered.

He was immortal.

Even if his body was completely gone, he still couldn't die.

He didn't know what he was supposed to do with that.

He had survived. But he was no longer human.

At first, Jhon tried to hide what he had become.

He wore a hood so people wouldn't immediately notice him.

People could still see him, though. Some people saw the strange black figure and ran away. Some hid their children. Others looked at him and simply didn't care.

Nobody really knew what he was.

And Jhon didn't know either.

Years continued to pass. The souls of the people Jhon had killed for the Devil eventually began hunting him. They had not forgotten him.

Sometimes he would see them.

Sometimes he would feel them.

Sometimes they would follow him.

They were reminders of the twenty-five years he had spent doing the Devil's work.

Jhon kept going anyway.

He had no choice.

The years continued.

The pain that had disappeared after the fire eventually returned. His soul could still feel pain. But his body still wouldn't come back.

There was nothing to heal.

He simply had to live with it.

Then something small changed.

One day, Jhon saw an older woman carrying groceries. She had several bags in her hands. As she walked, one of the bags slipped.

Before it could hit the ground, Jhon caught it. He handed it back to her.

“Thank you, young man,” the woman said in an old, tired voice.

“Yeah, okay,” Jhon replied.

It was nothing special. He had only caught a bag. But as he walked away, he noticed something.

It felt good.

For the first time in a long time, Jhon felt a little better. He felt almost normal.

Someone had simply needed help, and he had helped.

After that, Jhon started doing more good things.

They were small things. He helped people pick things up.

He helped people who were struggling.

He held doors open. He did little favors.

None of them were heroic on their own.

But every time he helped someone, his day felt a little better.

He became happier. One day, someone even offered him some food.

Jhon wasn't expecting it. The person simply asked if he wanted some.

It was such a normal thing. But to Jhon, it meant much more.

He felt like he was being treated like a human.

For someone who had spent years being feared, hunted, and treated like something that shouldn't exist, that feeling was almost impossible to describe.

He started doing more and more good things.

Eventually, people in the town began to recognize him.

They didn't know his name.

In fact, nobody knew his name.

Not even Jhon knew his own name anymore.

But people knew the mysterious figure who helped them.

In a strange way, he became a kind of local hero. He never called himself a hero.

He didn't even think he deserved the word.

He was just trying to do things that made him feel human again.

As Jhon's heart grew kinder, something else began to change.

Blue fire started coming from his eyes.

It wasn't ordinary fire.

It was calm.

It didn't behave like normal flames.

Jhon could control it.

It was a strange, chill kind of fire, and it burned with a bright blue glow.

People saw it. But by then, nobody in town really looked at him strangely anymore.

To them, he was just the strange fire guy who helped people.

One day, a little kid walked up to him.

The kid looked at the blue flames around Jhon's eyes.

“What's your name, fire guy?”

Jhon paused.

He thought about it.

“Umm... I don't have one.”

The kid thought for a second.

“I think a cool name would be Maxihu1.”

Jhon considered it.

“Not a bad name, kid.”

The kid's mother looked embarrassed.

“I am so sorry, sir.”

Jhon shook his head.

“It's okay, ma'am.”

The mother and her child walked away.

Jhon stayed where he was for a moment.

Then something clicked.

He looked at the child.

He remembered the fire.

The screaming.

The burning house.

The baby he had carried out through the window.

He suddenly realized who the child was.

It was the baby.

The same baby he had saved all those years ago.

The child who had unknowingly given him a name was the same child whose life had caused Jhon to lose his body in the first place.

Jhon stood there silently.

He didn't know his old name anymore.

He didn't have his old body.

He didn't have the life he once had.

But he had something else.

He had a new name.

Maxihu1.

And, somewhere inside that strange black soul, Jhon was still there.

⚡ Dude

Dude is Maxihu1's best friend. He has no powers, but he is immortal.

He wears his cool mask and says "dude" way too much.

Dude's Story

Dude grew up with a normal life. Compared to Maxihu1, his life was actually pretty good. He wasn't poor, and he had a home and parents who took care of him.

For most of his childhood, there was nothing especially strange about him. He was just a normal boy living a normal life.

Then, when Dude was sixteen, he got terrible news. His mom and dad had sadly passed away.

Dude was suddenly alone. He still had the house for a while, but without his parents there was nobody to help him keep it. After a few months, he could no longer keep up with the rent.

Dude lost his home and became homeless.

After a few days of living on the streets, Dude had had enough. He felt like he had lost everything: his home, his parents, and the life he used to have.

Eventually, Dude climbed up a skyscraper. He was about to jump.

But then someone arrived.

It was Maxihu1.

Maxihu1 was already in his soul form. He saw Dude standing there and immediately tried to stop him.

"Don't jump!" Maxihu1 said.

Dude looked at him. "Why not? I lost everything. My home. My parents."

Maxihu1 understood exactly what Dude was feeling.

"I KNOW HOW IT'S LIKE!" Maxihu1 said.

Dude was confused. "Wha-what?"

"I know how it is. I lost my body. I lost my humanity."

Maxihu1 explained that even though his life had been ruined and he had lost the person he used to be, he was still here.

"But I am still here. So maybe life isn't that bad."

Maxihu1 stayed with Dude and talked to him for about twenty-five minutes. Little by little, Dude started to believe that maybe he still had something to live for.

Dude didn't want to die anymore.

He had also made a friend.

Then, just when things seemed to be getting better, Dude slipped.

Maxihu1 reacted immediately. He used his power to save Dude, but he knew there was a chance Dude would not survive the fall normally.

Maxihu1 did something incredible. He gave Dude immortality, just like he had.

Dude survived.

But there was a problem. Dude's face was gone.

Maxihu1 had split the magic between them so that Dude would become immortal, but he didn't completely heal. Because of that, Dude survived without his normal face.

Dude eventually got a mask to hide what was left of his face. It was the cheapest and coolest mask he could find in the dollar store.

The mask became a normal part of Dude's life. He didn't have any special powers. He was just immortal, like Maxihu1.

Maxihu1 and Dude became close friends. They stayed together and helped each other through the years.

Dude also had a habit of saying the word "dude" a lot. He said it so much that Maxihu1 eventually started calling him Dude.

The name stuck.

As the years went by, their friendship grew stronger. They were both immortal, neither of them aged, and they had each other to rely on.

Dude was okay with what had happened to him as long as he had Maxihu1.

And that is the story of how Dude met Maxihu1 and became his best friend.
